How to play

One deal a day. Everyone in the world haggles with the same seller over the same item. The seller has a hidden floor price — your job is to get as close to it as possible.

  • 💬 You have 5 offers. Each one comes with a tactic.
  • 🧠 Every seller has a personality. Read their intro and their replies — the right tactic on the right person works wonders. The wrong one can blow up the deal.
  • 😡 Lowball too hard and they get angry. Too angry, and they walk. Repeat the same trick and it stops working.
  • 🤝 Close the deal anytime at their current price — or hold out for a better one.

Score = how much of the possible discount you captured, out of 100. Share your emoji grid, keep your streak alive.

New deal at midnight UTC. 🌙
