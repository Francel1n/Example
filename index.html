How it works

1

Your browser does the work

Modern browsers can read and write PDF files natively fast. We ship the PDF engine to you, not your files to us.

2

Nothing is transmitted

There is no upload endpoint. No server ever touches your document — there's no server-side processing at all.

3

No account, no limits

No sign-up walls, no "2 files per day", no watermarks, no premium tier. Static files are cheap to serve; free stays free.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really private? How can I check?

Yes — verifiably. Open DevTools (F12) → Network tab, then merge or split a file. You'll see the page load its own scripts and anonymous usage pings, but no request ever contains your document. You can even go offline after the page loads: the tools keep working.

Is there a file size or page limit?

Only your device's memory. Hundreds of pages are no problem on any modern laptop or phone. Very large scans (500 MB+) depend on your device's RAM, since your device is doing the work.

Why is it free? What's the catch?

There's no catch to fund. Classic PDF sites pay for servers that process your files, so they push subscriptions. Here your device does the processing and the site is static files — hosting costs almost nothing.

Do you support password-protected PDFs?

Not yet. Encrypted files are detected and refused with a clear message rather than mangled. Decryption support is on the roadmap.

Does it work offline?

Once a tool page has loaded, yes — processing is fully local. Full offline installation (PWA) is on the roadmap.
